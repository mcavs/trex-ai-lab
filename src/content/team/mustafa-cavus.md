bio_en: >-
  Mustafa Çavuş is an Associate Professor of Applied Statistics in the Department
  of Statistics at Eskişehir Technical University and a researcher with the MI2.AI
  group. His work focuses on explainable and trustworthy machine learning, with
  particular attention to predictive multiplicity and the Rashomon effect — the
  phenomenon of equally-good models that disagree on individual predictions. He
  combines applied statistics and hypothesis testing with modern interpretable-ML
  methods, applied to high-stakes problems in finance, education, sports, and
  predictive maintenance.

  zeka yöntemleriyle birleştirerek finans, eğitim, spor ve kestirimci bakım gibi
