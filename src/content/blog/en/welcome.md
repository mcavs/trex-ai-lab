We started **trex.ai (Trustworthy & Explainable AI Lab)** with a simple conviction: as machine learning shapes
more high-stakes decisions, the ability to inspect how and why a model decides
becomes as important as the prediction itself.

## What we work on

Our research spans interpretability, faithful explanations, and trustworthy AI.
We care about methods that hold up under scrutiny — not explanations that merely
look convincing.

## This blog

Team members will share research notes, paper walkthroughs, and tutorials here.
Every post is just a Markdown file — open a pull request and it goes live.
